About this map

Taste of Cement (Ziad Kalthoum, 2017) follows Syrian men building a high-rise in Beirut while their own cities are bombed. A curfew keeps them in the building's foundations after dark, and the film barely lets them speak. Its sound tells the story instead: drills and engines on the site keep turning into tanks, shells and helicopters.

How it was made

Each sound of war was picked out by watching the film. Three are paired with the building sound the film cuts to. The other four are paired with the construction moment that measures closest in tone, loudness and rhythm. The percentage compares those measurements after lining the two sounds up.

Limits

  • The page holds no audio from the film, only measurements of it. Playback is a rough sketch rebuilt from those numbers.
  • A high percentage means the measurements are close, not that the sounds are identical.
