          About this map

          The film

          • Taste of Cement: documentary, 2017, 85 minutes, directed by Ziad Kalthoum
          • Kalthoum defected from the Syrian army in 2013 and moved to Beirut, where he made the film
          • Syrian workers build a skyscraper in Beirut while their homes in Syria are shelled; they may not leave the site after 7 pm
          • The narrator's father was also a builder who worked in Beirut
          • The narration ends: “Since the end of the war Beirut keeps building” (Lebanon's civil war ran from 1975 to 1990)
          • Sound design: Sebastien Tesch and Ansgar Frerich

          Syrians in Lebanon

          • By April 2014, one in four people in Lebanon was a registered Syrian refugee
          • Nine in ten Syrian refugee families in Lebanon live in extreme poverty (2021)
          • By October 2014, at least 45 Lebanese towns had imposed curfews on Syrian refugees. One man was told he could not go out at night because he was Syrian, then stabbed three times
          • Between 27% and 47% of Syrian refugees in Lebanese studies have PTSD or screen positive for it

          How it was made

          • Every sound of war was picked out by watching the film
          • Pairs 2, 3 and 7: the building sound was also picked by watching. Pairs 1, 4, 5 and 6: by searching the soundtrack for the closest-sounding building moment
          • Overlap compares tone, loudness shape and level after lining the two sounds up. A random pair of moments from the film scores about 40–55%
          • “Other moments” are found by searching every second of the soundtrack for similar tone and rhythm
          • The clips are short excerpts of the film's soundtrack, used for study; all rights belong to the film's makers
